Course overview

The Higher National Diploma in Computing is a two-year programme that gives you a strong foundation in computing theory and practical, industry-relevant skills. It is equivalent to the second year of a UK bachelor's degree.

You'll learn through hands-on labs, group projects and live briefs from local tech companies, guided by tutors with industry experience.

Software Development

Programming, OOP, web and app development.

Networking

Design, configure and secure modern networks.

Cyber Security

Threats, risk management and defensive practice.

Data & Databases

Database design, SQL and data analytics.

What you'll study

  • Programming
  • Networking
  • Professional Practice
  • Database Design & Development
  • Security
  • Planning a Computing Project

  • Computing Research Project
  • Business Intelligence
  • Cloud Computing
  • Application Development
  • Cyber Security Management

After completing the HND, students can top up to a BSc (Hons) in one year, locally or at a UK partner campus.

Entry requirements

  • HSC / A'Level with passes in at least 2 subjects, or National Diploma
  • English proficiency: IELTS 5.5 or Aurora internal English test
  • Mature applicants with relevant work experience are welcome
Not sure you qualify? Book a free counselling session.
