About Us

A UK education, delivered in Dhaka with care, ambition and global standards.

Our story

Learning that opens doors worldwide

At Aurora International, we believe every student deserves access to a world-class education. Our philosophy centres on curiosity, critical thinking and practical skills that prepare learners for a global career.

Founded in January 2010, Aurora works as a learning facilitator for UK partner universities and awarding bodies. We deliver their programmes locally, so students earn the same internationally recognised qualifications at a fraction of the cost of studying abroad.
